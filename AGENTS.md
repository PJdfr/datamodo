## Read and maintain PROJECT_STATE.md

[`PROJECT_STATE.md`](PROJECT_STATE.md) is the living handoff doc: what datamodo
is, its current state, recent changes, and next steps. **Read it at the start of
a session** to get oriented without prior context, and **update it at the end of
any task that changes the product's state** (move items between sections, add a
dated "Recent changes" line, adjust "Next steps"). Keep it a tight map, not a changelog.
