People, companies, invoices, amounts, dates, addresses and to-dos — from plain text, PDFs, screenshots and receipts. Each field comes with a confidence score so you know what to double-check.

Nothing. The Free plan is genuinely free with no card required — you'll have your first structured tables within minutes of forwarding something.
